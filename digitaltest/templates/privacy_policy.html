Privacy Policy

Version 1.0

1. Data Controller

The data controller responsible for your personal data is TheraHub, reachable at [CONTACT EMAIL].

2. What data we collect

  • First and last name
  • Date of birth
  • Birth number (stored as a one-way cryptographic hash — the original value is never saved)
  • Diagnosis (optional, special category health data under GDPR Art. 9)
  • Digital literacy test results (speed, accuracy, typing metrics)
  • Technology usage survey responses
  • Consent timestamp and IP address at the time of registration

3. Lawful basis for processing

We process your personal data on the basis of your explicit consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). For health-related data (diagnosis and cognitive test results), we rely on explicit consent under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR. You may withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account (see Section 6).

4. Purpose of processing

Your data is collected solely for the purpose of assessing digital literacy as part of a clinical evaluation. Results are reviewed by authorised medical staff at TheraHub only.

5. Recipients of your data

Your data is accessible to authorised medical staff at TheraHub. We use Render (render.com) as our hosting provider, acting as a data processor. No data is sold or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

6. Your rights

  • Right of access (Art. 15): Download a copy of your data at any time from the completion page.
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17): Delete your account and all associated data from the completion page.
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16): Contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL] to correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to portability (Art. 20): Your data export is provided in machine-readable JSON format.
  • Right to withdraw consent (Art. 7): Delete your account at any time; this constitutes withdrawal of consent.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You may complain to your national data protection authority.

7. Retention period

Patient records are retained for a maximum of 2 years from the date of registration, after which they are automatically deleted. Staff access logs are retained for a maximum of 90 days.

8. Security

All data is transmitted over HTTPS. Birth numbers are never stored in plain text; only a keyed cryptographic hash is kept. Access to patient data by staff is logged.

9. Contact

For any privacy-related requests or questions, contact us at [CONTACT EMAIL].
